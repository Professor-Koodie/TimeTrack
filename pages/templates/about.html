About Us

Elwatini (Pty) Ltd is a Black-owned and controlled South African company. We have vast experience in railway, civil, electrical and mechanical engineering, and project and construction management. Our service offerings include: Railway engineering studies Railway engineering design Civil, water and structural design Heavy and light current electrification Mechanical design Construction monitoring and management project management We are involved in all stages of projects from concept development and project implementation to operations and maintenance.



Quality Our quality management system is based on the principles of PMBOK®. Our quality policy ensures that we commit to meeting and exceeding the best practice standards within the industry. We aim to ensure that all our stakeholder expectations are met at every step of the project life cycle. We are in the process of acquiring ISO9001 Certification for Consulting Engineers.


Risk management We pride ourselves on our capability to coordinate the company's collective technical, project execution, construction, financial, and relationship-building skills using: Structured risk and project management tools considering the individual project needs. Qualitative and quantitative risk management processes supplemented by a broad risk management plan (RMP) to continuously evaluate projects risks from business development to completions and hand-over Our Vision To be a leading engineering consulting company providing professional engineering, design, project management, and construction management services to both private and public sector clients within the built environment.


Our Mission To deliver unprecedented business results to our clients through a commitment to quality; higher standards of safety and risk management; and continuous performance improvements in all projects.
